01 // THE AMBIGUITY PROBLEM

Spoken instructions can fail silently.

Conventional TTS engines read medical prescriptions literally. When 1-0-1 is pronounced as "one hundred one" instead of "one morning, zero afternoon, one night", patient safety is severely compromised.
